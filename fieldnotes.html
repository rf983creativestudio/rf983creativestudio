CONTENT & EDITORIAL CONCEPT

THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

FIELDNOTES is an imagined journal for curious makers and independent thinkers. Its voice should feel informed but never distant, with enough personality to make practical ideas enjoyable to read.

“Good ideas deserve clear words.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

A crisp editorial system pairs oversized serif headlines with compact sans-serif labels, short paragraphs and a generous reading column. A restrained ink, paper and botanical palette keeps the words in focus.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Forest ink · #20372F
Moss · #71816A
Paper · #F0EADC
Ochre · #C59A55

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The direction is shown through a journal landing page, an article opening and a small social quote card system. The writing samples are demonstration copy created for this fictional publication.
