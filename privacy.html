LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Pre-launch working draft. This policy reflects the website and practices described to date. The operator's legal name and business location have not been supplied. Verify all details and obtain appropriate legal review before accepting public enquiries or client work.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. About RF983

RF983 Creative Studio is an individual creative initiative preparing for launch in India. It is not currently represented as a registered company or other registered business entity. The initiative is operated by an authorised individual representative. The representative's legal name and business address have not yet been provided for publication.

2. Information collected

If you submit the project enquiry form, the information you choose to provide may include your name, email address, selected service, project goals, project description, preferred timeline, and optional audience, estimated budget and reference website. The form also records your acknowledgement of this privacy notice. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information through the form.

When you visit the website, the hosting provider and your browser may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as network and request information. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means the browser may contact Google's font service to retrieve those files.

3. How information is used

Enquiry information is used to review your request, understand the project requirements, communicate with you, prepare a response or quotation, and maintain records of the enquiry where reasonably necessary. It is not intended to be used for targeted advertising or sold to advertisers.

4. Form processing and service providers

The enquiry form uses Web3Forms to transmit and process submissions. The information is made available through the Web3Forms dashboard and is also sent to the studio's designated email inbox. Web3Forms handles information under its own terms, privacy policy and service settings. You can review its privacy information at web3forms.com/privacy.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and pages that load Google Fonts may request font files from Google. These providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy practices. RF983 does not currently intend to use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other analytics or advertising tracking tools. This statement should be updated if the website's configuration changes.

5. Access, sharing and security

Enquiry access is intended to be limited to the authorised operator through the Web3Forms dashboard and designated email inbox. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate the website and enquiry workflow. RF983 does not intend to sell enquiry information or disclose it to unrelated parties for their own marketing.

Reasonable safeguards will be used to protect enquiry information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion requests

Enquiry information is intended to be retained only for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond to the enquiry, manage a potential or agreed project, maintain appropriate records, or meet applicable legal obligations. No fixed retention period has currently been set. Copies may remain with service providers according to their own retention practices.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted by emailing rf983productions@gmail.com. Reasonable requests will be considered, subject to identity verification where appropriate, technical limitations and any legal or record-keeping obligations that require information to be retained.

7. Children

This website and its project enquiry process are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact the email address below so the request can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services, providers or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: rf983productions@gmail.com.
