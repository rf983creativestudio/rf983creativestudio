Information you provide

The RF983 project enquiry form asks for your name, email address, selected service, project goals, optional audience and budget details, preferred timeline and project description. This information is used to review and respond to your enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

Enquiry form submissions are transmitted to Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service, which forwards submissions to the email address associated with the form access key. Web3Forms may process and retain submitted information under its own privacy policy and service settings. Review its policy at https://web3forms.com/privacy before using the form. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Any additional third-party services should be listed here before they are enabled.

Your choices

For privacy questions or requests concerning an enquiry, use the contact route published on the website. This notice is a working draft and should be reviewed and completed with the studio’s verified contact details and applicable legal requirements before public launch.
