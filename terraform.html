BRAND IDENTITY CONCEPT

THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

TERRAFORM is a fictional landscape studio shaping outdoor spaces around place, climate and everyday use. Its identity should feel contemporary and grounded, with a clear relationship to the natural world.

“A place-led identity, rooted in its surroundings.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

Emerald, teal, olive and a mineral neutral form a flexible palette. Organic linework and a confident typographic wordmark create a system that can move between project boards, stationery and digital touchpoints.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Emerald · #17634C
Teal · #3C8B82
Olive · #9B9B5A
Limestone · #EEE9DC

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The identity is explored through a studio title panel, a project sheet and a simple stationery composition. All names and projects are fictional demonstration material.
