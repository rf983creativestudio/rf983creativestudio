PACKAGING DESIGN CONCEPT

THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

ROOT & RIND is an imagined botanical tea range inspired by ingredients, seasons and slow daily rituals. The packaging needs to feel fresh and distinctive while making the blend and product details easy to understand.

“Gathered from nature. Designed for everyday rituals.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

Leaf green, citrus yellow, warm ivory and a dark botanical tone create a lively but grounded shelf presence. Ingredient-inspired shapes and a clear label hierarchy help each blend feel related yet recognisable.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Leaf · #2F6545
Citrus · #E2C34B
Ivory · #F4EEDC
Bark · #604A36

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The study explores three blend labels, a front-of-pack hierarchy and a small launch announcement. It is a visual packaging concept, not a real food product or a claim about ingredients.
