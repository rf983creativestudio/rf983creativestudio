MUSIC & ARTWORK CONCEPT

THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

ORBIT is a fictional electronic music project exploring the feeling of movement through sound. The concept needed a visual identity that could feel atmospheric and energetic without relying on predictable music graphics.

“A visual pulse for sound that keeps moving.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

A deep midnight base is punctuated by electric blue, ultraviolet and a focused signal of coral. Circular forms, fine orbital lines and expressive typography suggest motion, repetition and sound in space.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Midnight · #11162C
Ultraviolet · #7657D5
Signal blue · #39A7D9
Coral · #F17C72

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The system is imagined across a digital release cover, track-listing artwork and a set of social announcement frames. The audio direction is described conceptually; no playable track or recorded release is being presented.
