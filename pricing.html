WHAT SHAPES A QUOTE

Clear scope.
Fair expectations.

Quotes are prepared around the actual work involved, including:

  • Type and number of deliverables
  • Complexity and creative direction
  • Required formats, sizes or platforms
  • Timeline and project urgency
  • Agreed revision rounds and any additional work

BEFORE WORK BEGINS

Know what you're agreeing to.

We first understand your requirements, then confirm the deliverables, fee, timeline, revision scope and handover details in writing.

No work is accepted on the basis of an unclear estimate. If the brief changes, any impact on cost or timing is discussed before additional work proceeds.
