A visual treatment for a fictional wellness launch, exploring bold type, simple transitions and a rhythmic sequence for short-form digital media.

MOTION GRAPHICS CONCEPT

THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

PULSE is an imagined wellness initiative focused on finding energy in everyday movement. The visual direction needs to feel active and optimistic without becoming visually noisy.

“Find your rhythm. Move with meaning.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

Indigo and violet form a strong base, with vivid pink and a clean highlight colour. Repeated type, cropped circles and directional lines suggest beats, transitions and forward momentum.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Indigo · #303C9B
Violet · #7956C9
Pulse pink · #EE6D9B
Cloud · #F3EAF2

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The study is presented as a set of storyboard frames for a short launch sequence, plus a title card and social cut-down direction. No finished animation or client campaign is being claimed.
