01 / BRAND IDENTITY CONCEPT

AUREL
The quiet luxury of scent.

A considered identity direction for a fictional independent fragrance house — built around warmth, restraint and the beauty of simplicity.

DISCIPLINEBrand identityFOCUSPackaging · Art directionSTATUSSelf-initiated concept
SCENT, IN ITS PUREST FORM

THE BRIEF

Quiet confidence.
Lasting presence.

AUREL is an imagined fragrance label for people drawn to thoughtful details rather than loud statements. The challenge was to create a visual world that feels elevated and tactile while remaining clear, contemporary and memorable.

The concept explores how a restrained wordmark, warm neutrals and carefully spaced typography can give a new fragrance house a recognisable point of view.

“Luxury is not excess. It is intention, expressed with care.”

THE CREATIVE DIRECTION

Warm materials.
Uncomplicated forms.

The visual language pairs editorial serif typography with a compact, understated information system. The imagined packaging uses deep brown, muted bronze and soft ivory to suggest a tactile, intimate experience.

01 / Restraint

Keep the layout quiet and let the name, material and proportions carry the identity.

02 / Warmth

Use earthy tones and soft contrast to create an inviting, human atmosphere.

03 / Clarity

Give every detail a purpose, from hierarchy to the smallest supporting line.

COLOUR & TYPOGRAPHY

A palette with depth.

The palette is intentionally compact, balancing grounded darks with a warm highlight and a soft paper-like neutral.

Espresso · #32251F
Bronze · #876B50
Sand · #D5C2A2
Ivory · #F4E9D7

Typography direction: expressive editorial serif for the brand name and campaign statements, paired with a clean sans-serif for product details and practical information.
