THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

COMMON TABLE is a fictional neighbourhood café whose personality is built around good food and easy conversation. Its social presence should feel welcoming, flexible and recognisable across different content types.

“Good food. Good company. Room for everyone.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

Coral, tomato red, soft peach and a dark coffee tone give the system warmth and appetite. Oversized words, simple shapes and a repeatable grid make room for menu highlights, announcements and community moments.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Tomato · #D94F3D
Coral · #F08B70
Peach · #F5C9A8
Coffee · #392B28

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The concept includes a feed rhythm, a menu feature tile and an event announcement layout. The café, its menu and all campaign content are fictional examples.
