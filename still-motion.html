THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

STILL / MOTION is a fictional visual campaign about noticing the small details in everyday life. The challenge is to make still imagery and moving frames feel like parts of one coherent story.

“Hold the moment. Let the story move.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

A warm, sunlit palette combines soft cream, terracotta, deep plum and muted olive. The layout uses large crops, deliberate negative space and short editorial captions to give each frame room.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Ink plum · #342333
Terracotta · #C66F50
Soft cream · #F2E8D8
Olive · #8B9165

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The concept is mapped across a campaign cover, a sequence of social frames and a simple video title card. These are art-direction studies rather than footage from a commissioned shoot.
