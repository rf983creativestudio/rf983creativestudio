SOLSTICE
Everyday light.

A campaign direction for a fictional contemporary lighting label, using calm compositions, product-led layouts and a warm visual palette.

CAMPAIGN DESIGN CONCEPT

THE BRIEF

Start with
the idea.

SOLSTICE is a fictional lighting label built around the role light plays in everyday rituals. The campaign should make practical objects feel desirable while staying calm, clear and human.

“A little light changes the whole room.”

CREATIVE DIRECTION

One idea.
A complete visual language.

A saturated teal and deep blue foundation is balanced with amber highlights and pale warm neutrals. Simple geometric light fields create a sense of glow without competing with the product.

01 / Character

Build a recognisable point of view with a consistent visual signature.

02 / Clarity

Keep hierarchy and communication clear across every application.

03 / Flexibility

Make the system adaptable to different formats without losing its identity.

COLOUR SYSTEM

A palette with personality.

Colour is used as a practical system: to establish mood, guide hierarchy and make the concept recognisable across formats.

Deep teal · #075B61
Blue hour · #203D70
Amber · #E6A64B
Mist · #E8E6D8

Typography direction: a distinctive display style for statements and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for labels, details and longer copy.

APPLICATION STUDIES

Designed to work in context.

The concept extends to a hero campaign poster, a product feature tile and a set of social launch graphics. Product imagery is represented through graphic composition rather than a real catalogue.
